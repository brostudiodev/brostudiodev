## Michał Nowakowski — Brostudio

**New here?** Read [Start here. The Map I Never Drew](https://automationbro.substack.com/p/start-here-the-map-i-never-drew) — what I run at home, what broke, and the order I would build it in again.

Automation and AI specialist. 20+ years in IT, more than ten of them in manufacturing and automotive as project manager, business analyst and automation analyst. ITIL 4 · PRINCE2 · Six Sigma.

I run my house and my daily life as one system: Home Assistant on a mini PC, around fifty sensors and meters, six Arduino Mega boards feeding it over MQTT through a component I wrote, Frigate doing camera analysis, and open-source models running locally on a second mini PC.

The rule I broke for two years and now do not: **you cannot automate chaos. Standardise, monitor, optimise, then automate — in that order.**
